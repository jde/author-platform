# author-platform

## State of the project

- Form: brainstorm
- Arc: build a scheduled, agent-run outreach system for one author
- Next session: pick any session from `notes/sessions.md`; Q1 (bios) is the gentlest start

A scheduled, agent-run system that helps Helen Lundström Erwin, author of *James' Journey*, *Sour Milk in
Sheep's Wool* and *The Lure of Water and Wood* (Powersimple), find the readers and colleagues her work
deserves. It runs on a schedule, uses her communication tools, and does three jobs:

1. **Find opportunities** — places, people, events, and moments where her work belongs.
2. **Build presence** — show up in those places with real contributions, not promotion.
3. **Make connections** — start and maintain relationships with people who can help.

## Start here
New computer? See `HANDOFF.md` for the one line to paste into Claude.
Opening this with an AI assistant? It reads `CLAUDE.md` and offers short sessions from `notes/sessions.md`.

## Principles

- The agent prepares; the author sends. Nothing goes out in her name without her approval.
- Every contact and opportunity lives in a plain file the author can read, not in the agent's memory.
- Relationships over reach. One real conversation beats a hundred follows.
- Every routine produces one short digest the author can act on in ten minutes.
