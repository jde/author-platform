## 250 words (press kit)
Helen Lundström Erwin was born and raised in Helsingborg, Sweden, and lives in New York City. Her historical fiction is based on archival research, including court and parish records.

Her first novel, *James' Journey* (2014), follows the son of a Virginia slave-owning family who studies in New York, becomes an abolitionist, and returns home to help enslaved people escape from his family's plantation. *Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool* (2021) is set in Lund, Sweden, around 1900: It follows Anette, an unwed mother who moves each time she becomes pregnant, and Hanna, whose café is a meeting place for suffragists after closing. Elise Ottesen-Jensen, later a co-founder of the International Planned Parenthood Federation, appears as a character. *The Lure of Water and Wood* (2023) is her first historical fantasy. It is narrated by Näcken and the Skogsrå, a water spirit and a forest spirit from Swedish folklore, during two centuries of sorcery trials in which people were accused of relationships with them.

In 2022 she built a setting from one of her novels in virtual reality, which she believes no other novelist had done. The Foremothers Café, based on Hanna's café in 1918, is a Swedish-American women's history museum and cultural center on the ENGAGE platform, hosting exhibitions, a Thursday fika, and an annual Foremothers Day on November 30. It was a 2026 Auggie Award finalist for Best Indie Creator and appears in the documentary *48 Hours in the Metaverse* and in Forbes.

She serves on the steering committee of the Historical Novel Society's New York chapter, is a member of XR Women and the Suffrage Historians Collaborative, and is Secretary of the Board of SWEA New York. She received a 2021 SWEA New York scholarship and a Puffin Foundation grant. She also writes children's books, including *Officer Helga Hedgehog Meets the New Neighbors*, and consults with writers on historical fiction.

## Speaker bio (conference programs, 80 words)
Helen Lundström Erwin is a historical novelist and the creator of The Foremothers Café, a women's history museum in virtual reality and a 2026 Auggie Award finalist. Her novels cover the Underground Railroad, the Swedish suffrage and reproductive-rights movements, and the 17th- and 18th-century sorcery trials, the last narrated by two figures from Swedish folklore. She has spoken at AWE USA, Immersive X, and the Historical Novel Society's New York chapter, and appeared in Forbes and *48 Hours in the Metaverse*.

## Café bio (XR contexts, 60 words)
The Foremothers Café is a Swedish-American women's history museum and cultural center in virtual reality, set in Lund, Sweden, in 1918 and based on a setting in Helen Lundström Erwin's novel *Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool*. Built with Christi Fenison, it runs on ENGAGE, holds 70 people, needs no headset, and is free. It was a 2026 Auggie Award finalist.
