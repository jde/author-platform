# Talk and podcast topics

Each is a title plus the two sentences that sell it. Pick the one that matches the host.

1. **The spirits were on trial too.** Sweden's sorcery trials prosecuted people for relations with Näcken and the Skogsrå. What happens when you let the accused creatures tell it?
2. **Blåkulla before Salem.** The Mora trial of 1669 was reported across Europe and shaped how Salem talked about witches two decades later. The Swedish story most English readers have never heard.
3. **Writing the Skogsrå without making her a monster.** Folklore's seductive forest woman as an ordinary participant in village life, not a warning label.
4. **A milkmaid, a café, and the vote.** How Swedish women organized after hours, and the real café culture that made it possible.
5. **Elise Ottesen-Jensen, the woman who taught Sweden to talk about sex.** From [Helen: her origin in one phrase] to founding RFSU in 1933 and co-founding the International Planned Parenthood Federation.
6. **I built my novel's café in virtual reality.** What it took, what it cost, what happened when strangers started showing up for fika.
7. **Museums without buildings.** The Foremothers Café as a case study in cultural institutions that exist only in VR.
8. **Research as plot.** How Virginia's slave laws rewrote James' Journey mid-draft, and why I let them.
9. **The family secret in the archive.** Writing historical fiction from your own relatives, on both sides of the Atlantic.
10. **Easter witches, Walpurgis fires, midsummer fiddlers.** The Swedish calendar as a folklore map.
11. **Historical fiction after the sources run out.** When the record is a trial transcript and a confession under duress.
12. **Self-publishing a 750-page novel.** Length, IngramSpark, and why I did not cut it. [Helen: only if she wants this on the list.]
