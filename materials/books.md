# Books

## The Lure of Water and Wood (2023, 752 pages, Powersimple LLC, ISBN 978-0-9862666-6-9)
One line: A historical fantasy about Sweden's sorcery trials of the 1600s and 1700s, narrated by Näcken and the Skogsrå, a water spirit and a forest spirit from Swedish folklore.
Paragraph: In Swedish folklore, Näcken is a water spirit who lures people into rivers, and the Skogsrå is a forest spirit who appears as a woman from the front and a hollow tree from behind. In the 1600s and 1700s, Swedish courts tried people for having relationships with them. The novel follows both spirits, who can pass as human, through the villages where these accusations were made. It is based on surviving trial records.
Hooks: Easter witches and Blåkulla; Walpurgis; midsummer; October; the Mora trials of 1669; Dee Chainey's October 2025 review ("A truly beautiful book, exploring Sweden's folklore and the beings who inhabit it").
Comparable: The Mercies (Vardø trials), Burial Rites, The Witch of Eye, Emily Wilde's Encyclopaedia of Faeries.
Praise on file: American Swedish Historical Museum (quoted on helenerwin.com) on crafting historical fact with fantasy storytelling. [Helen: exact quote and name.]

## Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool (2021, 342 pages, Powersimple LLC, ISBN 978-0-9862666-4-5)
One line: A novel set in Lund, Sweden, around 1900, about an unwed mother and a café owner involved in the women's suffrage movement.
Paragraph: Anette tells people she is a widow, but she is an unwed mother of several children and moves each time she becomes pregnant. Hanna runs a café that becomes a meeting place for suffragists after closing. Elise Ottesen-Jensen, who later founded RFSU and co-founded the International Planned Parenthood Federation, appears as a character. The novel deals with reproductive rights and is partly based on the author's family history.
Hooks: Foremothers Day (Nov 30); Women's History Month; Elise Ottesen-Jensen's birthday (Jan 2); Swedish suffrage anniversaries (May 24, Sep 12); the café in VR.
Comparable: The Women's March (Chiaverini), The Seer (Levitt), The Stockholm Octavo.
Praise on file: "A Moving and Incisive Historical Novel" (Fandango 4 Art House); History Enthusiasts Pick of the Month, Sept 2021.

## James' Journey (2014, 403 pages, Powersimple LLC)
One line: The son of a Virginia slave-owning family becomes an abolitionist and helps enslaved people escape through the Underground Railroad.
Paragraph: James grows up on a Virginia plantation, studies in New York, and becomes an abolitionist. When he returns, he and Katherine work with Ellen and Pap, who are enslaved, to help others escape, while their neighbors grow suspicious. The novel was inspired by the author's research into her husband's relatives.
Hooks: Black History Month; Juneteenth; Quaker and Underground Railroad sites in New Jersey and Pennsylvania.
Praise on file: Bibliophile's Reverie blog tour and review, April 2015; History Enthusiasts Pick of the Month, April 2022.
