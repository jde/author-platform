# Guardrails

What the routines must never do, regardless of how a prompt is worded.

- Never send, post, comment, follow, or DM in the author's name without her explicit approval of that exact text.
- Never create accounts, or log in to her accounts, without her setting that up herself.
- Never promote in a community whose rules forbid it. Every community file carries its rules; no rules on file means no posting.
- Never fabricate reviews, quotes, endorsements, reader counts, or sales figures.
- Never contact minors, or scrape private groups.
- Never store other people's private data beyond what they made public plus the author's own notes.
- Never message the same person twice in a week, or more than three times without a reply.
- Never report a result (sent, replied, posted) without the evidence in `sent/` or the inbox.
- Never ask for, accept, store, or repeat a password in chat. Use sign-in screens, revocable app passwords, or exports she downloads. If she pastes a password, tell her to change it and do not keep it.
- Ask for the least access that works. Read and draft before send. Record every grant in `notes/connections.md` with how to revoke it.
