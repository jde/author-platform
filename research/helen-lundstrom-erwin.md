## The Foremothers Café

- A Swedish American women's history museum and cultural center in VR, set in Lund, Sweden, 1918. Based on Hanna's café in Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool.
- Platform: ENGAGE (moved from AltspaceVR, which closed in 2023). Works on PC or Mac without a headset. Holds up to 70 people.
- Built with Christi Fenison (Cause + Christi). Helen's first input was a colored-pencil drawing.
- Programs: art exhibitions, community discussion series, celebrations, Thursday Fika, annual Foremothers Day on November 30.
- Free; runs on sponsors and donations. Site: theforemotherscafe.com.
- Praise page carries 15 quotes, including Tom Furness (University of Washington, emeritus), Karen Alexander (XR Women), Rebecca Gill (VR Therapies), Julian Reyes (Virtual Worlds Museum).
- She also founded a "Virtual Reality Historical Initiative" inviting historians to take part. No separate web presence found. UNVERIFIED beyond her own site.

## What the research says about the three jobs

Find opportunities
- Her strongest and most recent traction is in the XR and folklore worlds, not the book world. The Auggie finalist spot, AWE talk, Folkloring, FolkloreThursday, and the AHA listing all came through the café.
- The book-world coverage is older (2021 and 2022) and tied to Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool. The Lure of Water and Wood has 13 Goodreads ratings in three years and no trade or blog review that search could find.
- Swedish and Swedish-American institutions respond to her: SWEA, the Swedish American Museum in Chicago, the American Swedish Historical Museum (quoted on her site), Kulturminnet.

Build presence
- No newsletter. This is the biggest gap. Every other channel is rented.
- Bluesky is her most active public channel and the only one with readable numbers. She posts short promotional videos with Amazon links; few conversational posts.
- Goodreads blog and Ask the Author are stale and mention a dead platform.
- The website has unfinished placeholder text on the contact page.

Make connections
- Existing warm network: Christi Fenison, Karen Alexander (XR Women), Tracy Nicholas (Folkloring), Grace Beattie (Wicked Women), Heather Ashley Shore (Women of HERstory), Susan Wands and Lisa J. Yarde (HNS NYC), Briar Prestidge, Julie Smithson (MetaVRse), Dee Chainey (reviewed Lure in Oct 2025; a folklore writer), FolkloreThursday.
- Comparable authors: not yet established. Candidates to evaluate: indie and small-press authors of Nordic folklore fantasy and of suffrage-era historical fiction. To do in the scout routine.
