## Standing rules
- No more than three asks go out in any week. Quality over volume.
- Every ask is preceded by at least one no-ask touch to a person, or one specific compliment to an organization.
- Every reply gets a same-day thank-you draft.
- The Monday digest lists what is due this week from this file.
