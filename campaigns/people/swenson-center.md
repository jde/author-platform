# Swenson Swedish Immigration Research Center, Augustana College

## Who
Research center at Augustana College, Rock Island, Illinois. Runs the Nordic Book Club on Zoom, second Tuesday of the month, 6 to 7 pm Central, coordinated by Lucas Henry.

## Why they matter
A monthly Zoom book club that reads Nordic and Nordic-American authors and explicitly invites publishers to send books for consideration. Low effort, exactly the right readers, and a gateway to Augustana's Swedish-American network.

## What we know (facts with sources)
- bookclub@swensoncenter.org; general swensoncenter@augustana.edu, (309) 794-7204 (swensoncenter.org/bookclub).
- Coordinator Lucas Henry, ethnomusicologist, former Visiting Assistant Professor of Scandinavian Studies.
- Upcoming meetings Nov 10 and Dec 8, 2026. Readers suggest titles by form; publishers may mail a copy for consideration.
- Free to members and students, $10 for others (older Augustana news item; verify).
- Augustana's library hosts a research guide on Swedish and Swedish-American literature.
