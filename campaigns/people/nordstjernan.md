# Nordstjernan

## Who
Swedish-American newspaper, founded 1872, published in New York by Swedish News, Inc. Mostly English. Mix of news, traditions, food, trivia and history.

## Why they matter
The one Swedish-American outlet with national reach that has never covered Helen, as far as search shows. Its readers are the audience for *Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool*, the café, and the folklore calendar, and the paper publishes Swedish-history pieces.
