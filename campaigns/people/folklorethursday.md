# FolkloreThursday

## Who
Hashtag day (Thursdays, Bluesky and elsewhere) and online magazine of folklore articles by creatives and academics; runs a Creative Corner. Hosted Helen's lecture on The Lure of Water and Wood in the Cultural Futures Hub on Spatial.io, February 19, 2026, with an accompanying folklore art exhibition. The Hub closed June 18, 2026. Channel: folklorethursday.bsky.social, site contact. [Helen: who she dealt with in February, their email.]

## Why they matter
The largest weekly folklore audience online. An article on the magazine and a regular presence on the Thursday hashtag put the book in front of exactly its readers, every week, at no cost.
