### Post 1, #WyrdWednesday, the Skogsrå
In Swedish folklore the Skogsrå is a forest spirit who looks like a beautiful woman from the front and like a hollow tree or a fox's tail from behind. In the Swedish sorcery trials, men were tried for having met her in the forest. #WyrdWednesday #Folklore

### Post 2, #FolkloreThursday, Mora 1669
In Mora, Sweden, in 1669, children told the court that neighbors had flown them to Blåkulla to feast with the Devil. Their accounts matched partly because they were asked the same questions in the same order. Reports of the trial spread across Europe and were still cited when the Salem trials began. #FolkloreThursday #WitchTrials

### Post 3, #MythologyMonday, Näcken
In Swedish folklore Näcken is a water spirit who plays the fiddle in streams and lures people into the water. In some trial records, a man's claim that Näcken had taken him was offered to explain where he had been overnight. #MythologyMonday #SwedishFolklore
