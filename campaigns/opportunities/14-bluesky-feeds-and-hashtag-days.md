### Post 1, #WyrdWednesday, the Skogsrå
From the front, the Skogsrå is the most beautiful woman in the parish. From behind she's a hollow tree, or a fox's tail she forgot to hide. Men swore in Swedish courts that she met them in the forest, and the court tried them for it. #WyrdWednesday #Folklore

### Post 2, #FolkloreThursday, Mora 1669
Mora, Sweden, 1669. Children told the court their neighbors flew them to Blåkulla to feast with the Devil. The confessions matched because the questions came in the same order every time. Reports crossed Europe and were still being quoted when Salem started asking children questions. #FolkloreThursday #WitchTrials

### Post 3, #MythologyMonday, Näcken
Näcken sits naked in the stream and plays the fiddle until you walk in. That's the folklore. In the court records he's something stranger: an alibi. "He took me" explained where a man had been all night, and the court believed it enough to kill for it. #MythologyMonday #SwedishFolklore

### Post 7, Lucia, December 13
Lucia morning in Helsingborg meant candles before daylight and saffron buns you were not supposed to eat yet. In the 1700s the night before was Lussinatta, when the Lussi and her train rode the dark. Light against the longest night, by design. God Lucia. #SwedishTraditions #Folklore
