# American Fiction Awards 2027

## Goal
Enter *The Lure of Water and Wood* in two categories before the early-pricing deadline, for a finalist or winner credential usable on the cover, the press kit, and in pitches.

## Why her
Eligible books are 2022 through 2027, which admits a November 2023 novel; most other awards do not. Categories include Fantasy: Historical and Historical Fiction. Low prestige, low cost, and a finalist line is still a line.

## Contact and mechanism
- Site: americanbookfest.com/americanfictionawards.html
- Final deadline: May 31, 2027. Winners and finalists announced July 2027.
- Fee: $77 per title per category until October 31, 2026, then $89. Two categories at early price: $154. Payment via PayPal in US dollars.
- Entry by email: pay online, then email the completed entry form (Word or PDF from the site) and a PDF of the book to abf@amerbookfest.com, including the email used for payment.
- Entry by mail: one copy per category, Page One form per title, check to "ABF." Entered books are not returned.

### Cover note, if the form has a description field
*The Lure of Water and Wood* is a historical fantasy set in Sweden between 1600 and 1800, narrated by Näcken and the Skogsrå, the folklore beings whose supposed lovers were tried for sorcery. It is built on trial records from Mora, Älvdalen and the Svea court, with the folklore treated as the accused experienced it. 752 pages, Powersimple LLC, 2023. Author: Helen Lundström Erwin, Historical Novel Society New York chapter steering committee, 2026 Auggie Award finalist.
