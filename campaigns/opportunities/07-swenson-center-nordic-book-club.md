# Swenson Center Nordic Book Club (Augustana College)

## Goal
Get *Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool* or *The Lure of Water and Wood* chosen as a monthly selection, with Helen joining the Zoom meeting.

## Why her
The Swenson Swedish Immigration Research Center's Nordic Book Club meets online on the second Tuesday of each month, 6 to 7 p.m. Central, and reads books by Nordic authors or on Nordic and Nordic-American topics. Both novels qualify. The page says publishers may offer books by contacting the center or mailing a copy, which gives Powersimple a direct route. Readers may also suggest titles through a form on the page.
