Hi Daniel,

I listened to your episode with Ivo and Mags Havgar about *Tungl*. [Helen: one point from the episode.] I'd like to suggest an episode on what happened to Nordic folk beliefs in the 1600s, when they ended up in court.

Swedish courts in that period tried people for having relations with Näcken, a water spirit, and the Skogsrå, a forest spirit. At Mora in 1669, children's testimony about being taken to Blåkulla led to executions, and reports of the trial spread across Europe. My novel *The Lure of Water and Wood* is narrated by Näcken and the Skogsrå during those trials.

As far as I can tell, you haven't had a novelist on the show yet. I'm available most evenings, Eastern time.
