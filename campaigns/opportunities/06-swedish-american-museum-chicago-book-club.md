# Swedish American Museum, Chicago, online book club

## Goal
A return visit to the museum's Zoom book club, this time for *The Lure of Water and Wood*, scheduled for October 2027 (spooky season) or the Saturday before Foremothers Day, with the museum store stocking the book.

## Why her
The museum hosted Helen on January 29, 2022 for *Sour Milk in Sheep's Wool*, so the door is already open. The club meets the last Saturday of each month at 2 p.m. Central on Zoom and reads Nordic books sold in the museum store (recent picks: *Winter Water* by Susanne Jansson, *When the Cranes Fly South* by Liza Ridzén). It is the warmest institutional lead on the list.
