# Three Ravens Podcast, "Local Legends"

## Goal
A Local Legends interview that pairs Swedish forest and water spirits with their English and Welsh cousins, with *The Lure of Water and Wood* as the book.

## Why her
Three Ravens is hosted by Eleanor Conlon and Martin Vaux, recording in rural Sussex. Local Legends is their Saturday interview strand with "authors, historians, folklorists and storytellers," and recent guests include Mark Norman (The Folklore Podcast), Delyth Badder, Sacha Coward (*Queer As Folklore*) and Tony Walker. The show is regional by design, so the pitch is comparative: what the Skogsrå and Näcken share with the hollow-backed and river-dwelling spirits of Britain. Booking Mark Norman first (campaign 02) makes this pitch warmer.
