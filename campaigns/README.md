# Campaigns

One file per opportunity in `opportunities/`, one per person or organization in `people/`. Each is a
plan, a timeline, the contact and submission route, and first drafts of every message. All drafts are
in Helen's voice from `materials/voice.md`, built on `materials/`. Nothing is sent until she approves
the exact text. Square brackets are hers to fill.

Opportunity file sections: Goal · Why her · Contact and mechanism · Timeline · Materials · Drafts · Follow-up rule · Status.
People file sections: Who · Why they matter · What we know · Touch plan (dated, no-ask touches first) · Drafts for each touch · Stop rule · Status.

`master-timeline.md` is the single calendar across every file.
